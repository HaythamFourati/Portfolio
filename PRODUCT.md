# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and engineering leads evaluating Haytham Fourati for front-end / WordPress roles, plus prospective freelance clients. They land here from a LinkedIn link, a job application, or a referral, usually skimming many portfolios in one sitting. They have seconds to decide "is this person sharp and different?" Their job-to-be-done: quickly verify real, shipped work and form a confident read on how this developer thinks and works.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio home page that wins the recruiter in the first fold. It exists to make one argument unmistakably: Haytham is an **AI-native front-end developer** who ships production-grade websites dramatically faster by treating AI as a pair-programmer while owning the craft, taste, and quality himself. Success = the visitor remembers the person (not the template), trusts the shipped work, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

**AI-native, precise, quietly confident.** Voice is a senior engineer who lets the work talk: direct, technical, no fluff, a dry wit. Speaks in the vocabulary of the craft (prompts, pipelines, shipping, diffs) without cosplaying. Emotional goal: the visitor should feel they're looking at someone operating a level above the generic portfolio crowd — fast, modern, and in control of the tools, not impressed by them.

## Anti-references

- The generic "AI-modern" portfolio: near-white background, violet/lime gradient blobs, a tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow (`PORTFOLIO` / `EXPERTISE`) above every section, identical icon-card grids, and a hero-metric strip (5+ / 50+ / 100%). This is the *previous* version of this very site and the exact thing to escape.
- SaaS landing-page template look (Linear/Vercel clone with no point of view).
- Monospace-everything "hacker" cosplay where mono is decoration, not function.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif italic + drop caps + ruled columns) — wrong register for a developer.

## Design Principles

- **Show the method, don't claim it.** The interface itself should feel AI-augmented and engineered — the medium proves the message. Demonstrate "10x with AI" through a crafted experience, not a bullet that says "AI-powered".
- **Operator, not spectator.** Frame Haytham as someone who *drives* AI to outcomes. AI is the pair; the taste, review, and shipping are his.
- **Real work over adjectives.** Every claim is backed by a named, linked, shipped client site.
- **Earn the dark.** A dark console canvas is a deliberate scene (a developer's working environment at the terminal), not a default — it must feel inhabited and precise, never decorative.
- **Fast is the proof.** Performance, responsiveness, and reduced-motion fallbacks are part of the argument: a developer who ships fast ships a fast site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark canvas (near-white ink, muted text bumped toward ink, never light gray "for elegance"). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for the typed-prompt hero and scroll choreography (content visible by default, motion enhances). Keyboard-navigable, semantic landmarks, visible focus states, honest alt text on all imagery.
